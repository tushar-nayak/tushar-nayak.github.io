Our systematic review on using artificial intelligence and machine learning to predict tumor recurrence in high-grade gliomas has been accepted at [Neurosurgical Review](https://link.springer.com/journal/10143). The work was done with Dr. Trent Kite, Professors [Aswin Sankaranarayanan](https://scholars.cmu.edu/4356-aswin-sankaranarayanan) and [Pulkit Grover](https://www.cmu.edu/bme/People/Faculty/profile/pgrover.html), and [Dr. Matthew Shepard](https://findcare.ahn.org/Matthew-J-Shepard).
