I am a staff scientist at the [Surgical Biomechanics Lab](https://surgbiomech.uchicago.edu) under Dr. Luka Pocivavsek at the University of Chicago, and a research collaborator with the University of Pittsburgh's Surreality Lab. I recently completed my M.S. in Biomedical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University.

I build computer vision and learning systems that recover useful anatomy, motion, and deformation from sparse, noisy, clinically constrained data, spanning geometric vision, 3D reconstruction, and physics-informed deep learning for image-guided intervention.

I am currently applying to PhD programs. My interests are in sparse-view 2D–3D reconstruction, deformable anatomical modeling and registration, neural implicit and Gaussian representations, graph-based geometric learning, and physics-informed methods for image-guided intervention. More on my [research interests and project work](/research/), or see my [CV](https://github.com/tushar-nayak/cv) for the complete record.
