medical imaging / surgical robotics / 3d vision

Geometry-aware vision for image-guided robotic surgery.

I am a scientist at the SurgBioMech / Pocivavsek Lab at the University of Chicago. I develop computer vision and learning systems that recover useful 3D structure from sparse, noisy, and clinically constrained data.

Current work: endovascular robotics, fluoroscopy-to-CTA registration, sparse-view reconstruction, and clinically grounded biomedical vision.
