Tushar Nayak

building geometry-aware vision systems for image-guided robotic surgery

Geometry-aware vision for image-guided robotic surgery.

I am a staff scientist at the Surgical Biomechanics Lab under Dr. Luka Pocivavsek at the University of Chicago, and a research collaborator with the University of Pittsburgh's Surreality Lab. I recently completed my M.S. in Biomedical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, where I build computer vision and learning systems that recover useful anatomy, motion, and deformation from sparse clinical data.

FluoroGRID: Real-Time 3D Vascular Deformation from Monocular Fluoroscopy

Sparse-View Cardiac Reconstruction with Neural Implicit Representations

Completes 3D left-ventricular geometry from three annotated echocardiography sections, scored only on the voxels the model never observed.
