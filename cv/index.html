Selected Research Projects

  • 2025-2026
    Sparse-View Cardiac Reconstruction with Neural Implicit Representations
    • Coordinate-based neural implicit reconstruction completing dense 3D left-ventricular geometry from three sparse 2D echocardiography sections, scored only on unobserved voxels, achieving 0.936 Dice and 0.880 IoU on MITEA.
    • Matched comparison against an explicit Gaussian occupancy field under identical observations (0.902 Dice, 0.821 IoU), with mesh and topology diagnostics distinguishing genuine shape error from label artifacts.
  • 2026-present
    Deformable 3D Reconstruction of Live Endoscopic Surgical Scenes
    • Prototyping deformable 3D Gaussian Splatting for dynamic monocular endoscopic reconstruction with depth/pose initialization, differentiable rendering, and deformation fields.
    • Exploring semantic feature alignment for open-vocabulary querying of reconstructed surgical scenes.
  • 2026
    Geometric Multi-View Breast Cancer Detection
    • Geometric alignment and dual-view correspondence framework for paired CC/MLO mammography, integrating lesion segmentation, localization, registration, and cross-view attention.
    • Reduced cross-view lesion-centroid discrepancy by 99.1%, enabling spatially aligned multi-view representations for malignancy classification.
  • 2025
    Sparse-View Coronary Vessel Reconstruction
    • Research prototype for sparse-view 3D coronary vessel reconstruction from angiography projections using Gaussian geometry, differentiable rendering, and PINN-based hemodynamic regularization. Now archived.
  • 2025-present
    Open Horizon Robotics Perception Curriculum
    • Authoring open-source computer vision curriculum covering classical vision, deep learning, 3D vision, geometry, and perception physics.
